# SideQuest Terms and Agreement


*Organized by:* AWS Learning Club – NU Dasmariñas

SideQuest is an interactive activity where participants complete assigned challenges by *taking photos and submitting them to the AWS Learning Club – NU Dasmariñas (AWSLC-NUD)*.


By participating in SideQuest and submitting a photo, you agree to the following terms.


## 1. Photo Submission


Participants may be asked to take and submit photographs as proof that they have completed a SideQuest challenge.

Participants are responsible for ensuring that the photos they submit are appropriate and related to the assigned challenge.

Photos that are unrelated, intentionally misleading, offensive, unsafe, or inappropriate may be rejected.


## 2. People Appearing in Photos

Before intentionally taking or submitting a recognizable photo of another person, participants should ask for that person's permission.

Do not photograph individuals who do not want to be included.

Participants should be especially careful when taking photos involving people who are not participating in SideQuest.


## 3. Privacy

Submitted photos may contain personal information, including a participant's appearance or other identifiable individuals.

AWSLC-NUD will use submitted photos primarily for:

* Verifying completion of SideQuest challenges;
* Recording participant progress;
* Administering points, achievements, or rewards;
* Resolving disputes regarding challenge completion; and
* Operating and improving SideQuest.


Participants should avoid including unnecessary private or sensitive information in their photos.

This includes IDs, passwords, personal documents, private conversations, addresses, financial information, or other confidential information.

## 4. Use of Submitted Photos

Submitting a photo to SideQuest gives AWSLC-NUD permission to access and review the photo for purposes related to administering SideQuest.


*Submitting a SideQuest photo does not automatically give AWSLC-NUD permission to publicly post the photo on social media, advertisements, promotional materials, or other public platforms.*


If AWSLC-NUD wishes to publicly use a participant's submitted photo for promotional purposes, permission should be obtained separately when appropriate.

## 5. Acceptable Photos


Participants must not submit photos containing:

* Nudity or sexually explicit content;
* Harassment, bullying, or discriminatory content;
* Illegal activities;
* Dangerous or intentionally harmful activities;
* Private information belonging to another person;
* Content intended to embarrass or harm another individual;
* Photos taken in locations where photography is prohibited; or
* Any other content considered inappropriate for a university or community activity.

AWSLC-NUD may reject or remove submissions that violate these rules.

## 6. Safety

Participants should never place themselves or another person in danger simply to complete a SideQuest challenge.

Participants must follow university rules, venue rules, posted restrictions, and instructions from organizers or university personnel.

A SideQuest challenge should never be interpreted as permission to enter restricted areas, disturb classes or activities, damage property, or ignore safety requirements.

## 7. Authenticity and Fair Play

Photos should represent the participant's actual completion of the assigned SideQuest.

Participants must not intentionally submit another person's photo, reuse an unrelated photo, fabricate evidence, or manipulate a submission in a way that falsely represents completion of a challenge.

AWSLC-NUD may request clarification or additional proof when necessary.

## 8. Storage of Photos

Submitted photos may be temporarily stored as necessary to operate SideQuest, verify submissions, maintain activity records, and resolve concerns.

AWSLC-NUD should retain submitted photos only for as long as reasonably necessary for the activity and related administrative purposes.

Participants may contact the organizers regarding concerns about a submitted photo or the handling of their information.

## 9. Removal of Submissions

AWSLC-NUD may remove or reject photos that:

* Violate these terms;
* Do not satisfy the requirements of the challenge;
* Include inappropriate or prohibited content;
* Raise legitimate privacy or safety concerns; or
* Were submitted through unfair or fraudulent means.

## 10. Participant Responsibility

Participants are responsible for the photos they take and submit.

By submitting a photo, the participant confirms that, to the best of their knowledge:

* They are allowed to submit the photo;
* The photo does not intentionally violate another person's privacy;
* Any recognizable individuals intentionally included in the photo have been informed when appropriate; and
* The submission complies with the SideQuest rules.

## 11. Changes to SideQuest

AWSLC-NUD may modify challenges, submission requirements, deadlines, or these terms when reasonably necessary for safety, privacy, fairness, or event operations.

Important changes affecting participants will be communicated whenever reasonably possible.

## Agreement

By selecting *"I Agree"* and participating in SideQuest, you confirm that you have read and understood these terms.

By submitting a photo, you also acknowledge that the photo will be received and reviewed by AWSLC-NUD for the purposes of administering SideQuest.

*Take the quest. Capture the moment. Respect everyone's privacy.*
